Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • A sump pump should be tried out before storms arrive, ideally with a backup battery installed.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • In basements and other low areas, keep documents and valuables up on shelves.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.

Warning Signs for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • Water is coming through a ceiling, light fixture or electrical outlet.
  • The water keeps running, and the shutoff valve is out of reach or nowhere to be found.
  • The house was flooded when you walked in, with no telling how many hours it had been going.
  • The breaker box, a running appliance or cords along the floor sit in the path of the water.
